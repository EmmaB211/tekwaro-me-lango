Community authority comes first

Every record has
a review path.

Nothing submitted is published automatically. Editors must document provenance, permissions, review and access restrictions before publication.

  1. 01Draft

    Work stays private and unpublished.

  2. 02Submitted

    Contributor provides background and permission details.

  3. 03Under review

    Editor checks completeness, sources and rights.

  4. 04Cultural verification

    Relevant elders or language specialists review the material.

  5. 05Approved

    Authorized reviewer confirms the content and audience.

  6. 06Published

    Only approved, permitted public material appears online.

Restricted, disputed or withdrawn items remain offline. Read the editorial review framework and privacy and cultural rights guidance.
